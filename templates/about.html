About us

Making wedding discovery simpler for Telugu families.

ShubhaVedika is a wedding discovery platform created to help families explore venues and wedding service professionals in one place. From Kalyana Mandapams and photographers to catering, bridal makeup, decorators and more, we bring useful options together to make the search easier.

Our purpose

Planning a wedding involves many decisions and conversations. ShubhaVedika aims to make the discovery process more organised by helping couples and families browse services by category and location, review available business details, and contact vendors directly.

For families and wedding professionals

Families can explore wedding-related services across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Wedding professionals can create a business listing and present their services, photos and contact information to people planning their celebrations. Families should confirm availability, pricing and service terms with each business before booking.
